Adds a Robber class "BountyHunter"

Primary Ability: Cloaking Device
- Turn invisible for 10 seconds. (Turrets and AI will still be able to shoot at you.) If you hold weapons masks or bags, they will be visible!
- The cooldown for Primary is 40 seconds, and it is active for 10 seconds.

Secondary Ability: Bounty
- You have to kill a random employee on the map. When you kill the target, you receive a new bounty target and a $5,000 reward! Watch out the Cops also see your Target!
- The Target will be pinged every 10 seconds, and the ping will be visible for everyone. (You should only get the money if the BountyHunter kills the Target.)
